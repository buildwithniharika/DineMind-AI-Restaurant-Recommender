## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet confidence, tailored discernment, and sensory warmth. Designed for an intimate AI restaurant concierge, the aesthetic rejects the frantic, transactional nature of traditional delivery aggregators and the synthetic violet cliches of generic AI interfaces. Instead, it evokes the physical presence of a Michelin-starred maître d': composed, knowledgeable, discreet, and refined.

The visual direction marries warm architectural minimalism with tactile editorial sensibility. Key design principles:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Replacing harsh digital whites with organic limestone, fine linen, and warm travertine tones.
- **Restraint Over Noise:** Generous negative space, disciplined layouts, and an intentional absence of aggressive badges, flashing banners, or push notifications.
- **Analog Craft in Digital Form:** Subtle, low-contrast hairline borders, soft clay accents, and refined typographical hierarchies that mirror bespoke culinary menus and hospitality journals.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from raw natural materials—sun-baked terracotta, dried sage, lime plaster, and charred oak. 

- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Warm Clay):** Used sparingly for critical points of intentional interaction: reservation confirmations, primary action buttons, key date selectors, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#607264` - Muted Sage):** Represents calm status, dietary badges, curated chef selections, and soft affirmative feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#8A7D70` - Warm Taupe):** Serves auxiliary roles such as subtle icons, secondary metadata, and non-intrusive timestamps.
- **Neutral Core:**
  - **Surface Canvas (`#FBF9F6`):** The primary serene, warm off-white foundation.
  - **Surface Layer 1 (`#F6F4F0`):** Elevated cards, input fills, and sheet backgrounds.
  - **Surface Layer 2 (`#EFECE6`):** Nested details, active filters, and subtle hover states.
  - **Hairline Borders (`#E9E4DC`):** Delicate separation lines that define structure without visual weight.
  - **Deep Charcoal Typography (`#1C1A17` and `#2D2A26`):** Soft yet deeply legible ink tones that provide high contrast against cream backgrounds without the harsh glare of pure black.
  - **Muted Body (`#6B655D`):** Editorial secondary copy, tasting note descriptions, and addresses.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides a contemporary balance between geometric clarity and humane warmth. Its sculpted curves evoke subtle hospitality menus while preserving optimal legibility on digital viewports.

- **Headlines:** Set in Medium (`500`) and SemiBold (`600`) weights with tight negative letter-spacing to impart an editorial, measured tone. Avoid extra-bold or aggressive black weights.
- **Body Copy:** Generously spaced line heights (`1.65` to `1.85`) prevent visual fatigue and support long-form narrative recommendations, tasting menus, and culinary descriptions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Formatted in uppercase or small tracking weights with subtle letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for categorical breadcrumbs, neighborhood indicators, and dietary specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an architectural rhythm centered around expansive white space and uncluttered focal zones. 

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet screens, folding into an adaptive single-column flow on mobile.
- **Max Canvas Width:** Capped at `72rem` (`1152px`) for content layouts to preserve an intimate reading experience that mirrors high-end editorial publications.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous section spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) gives conversational AI prompts and restaurant discovery cards room to breathe. Component interiors rely on structured padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), eliminating cramped UI edges.
- **Adaptive Reflow:** 
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** `1.25rem` screen margins, conversational input fixed with bottom safe-area elevation, cards collapse to horizontal swipe trays or stacked editorial blocks.
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Split-view layouts paired with conversational AI concierge streams alongside contextual dining dossiers, floorplans, and culinary dossiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter definition rather than aggressive skeuomorphic drops.

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is constructed by resting `#F6F4F0` surfaces onto the `#FBF9F6` canvas, backed by a subtle border tone (`#E9E4DC`). Secondary modules within cards use `#EFECE6`.
- **Borders over Shadows:** Surfaces maintain a `1px` structural outline in `#E9E4DC`. This creates clean architectural separation reminiscent of premium stationery.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows:** When an interactive element floats (such as the conversational query drawer, booking sheet, or date picker), use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.02);`
- **Zero Glare:** Disallow harsh black drop shadows, saturated glows, or high-contrast drop borders.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, rounded visual language that feels soft to the touch while retaining architectural precision.

- **Panels & Cards:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) establishes gentle, protective contours around restaurant vignettes, AI recommendations, and tasting notes.
- **Interactive Inputs & Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) creates tactile containment for form controls, filters, and chat dialogue containers.
- **Pills & Micro-indicators:** `rounded-full` (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for status indicators, conversational chips, dietary tags, and small pill buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Warm Clay (`#C85A32`) with `#FFFFFF` text. Minimal hover transition introducing a subtle warmth shift (`#B64E2A`). Border radius: `rounded-lg`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #E9E4DC`, text `#1C1A17`. On hover, background transitions to `#F6F4F0`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#2D2A26`, zero borders. Hover adds a soft wash of `#F6F4F0`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact `rounded-full` elements. Unselected state features `#F6F4F0` fill, `1px solid #E9E4DC` border, and `#6B655D` label text.
- Selected state shifts to `#1C1A17` fill with `#FBF9F6` text or Warm Sage (`#607264`) for dietary/atmosphere preference filters.

### Input Fields & Concierge Query Bar
- High-comfort, generously sized inputs. Background `#F6F4F0` surrounded by `1px solid #E9E4DC`.
- Focus state softens the border to `#C85A32` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.12)`. No abrupt high-contrast outlines.
- Floating conversational prompt container anchored at viewport bottom features an integrated send trigger and curated prompt suggestion pills above the field.

### Restaurant & Dossier Cards
- Grounded in `#F6F4F0` with a subtle border (`#E9E4DC`) and `rounded-xl` shape.
- Generous internal padding (`1.5rem`).
- Photography features warm, desaturated color grading with subtle corner radii (`rounded-lg`) inset within the parent card.
- Metadata (neighborhood, cuisine genre, Michelin status) arranged cleanly with generous letter-spaced labels.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision geometry with soft corners. Inactive: `1.25rem` square/circle, border `1.5px solid #E9E4DC`, background `#FBF9F6`.
- Active: Background fills with `#C85A32`, featuring an off-white micro checkmark or center pip.

### Conversational AI Stream & Thought Nodes
- AI interaction renders as a quiet dialogue. User prompt bubbles align right in `#EFECE6`.
- Concierge responses align left directly on the `#FBF9F6` surface (borderless), prioritizing clear typography, curated restaurant card embeds, and warm sage culinary tags.
